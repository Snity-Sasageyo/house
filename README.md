# The House at the 47 Harrow Lane

A short haunted exploraation game where a family from 1987 is vanished and the police found nothing.

And you walk in and the door behind you locks in.

## What this is 

It is a small, self-contained horror exploration site where you move through a house, examine objects, collect keepsakes and unlock truth in the basement

It is meant to be:

- short
- replayable
- quiet
- strange
- and easy to run
